How to use this site

Three ways in

  1. 1

    Read the section pages

    Open any week to get a structured walkthrough — overview, math, worked examples, glossary and pitfalls. Use the Contents rail on the left to jump within a section, or the search in the top bar (press /) to find a concept across the whole course.

  2. 2

    Drill with flashcards

    Once a section feels familiar, switch to the flashcard view to test recall. Cards are tagged easy / medium / hard and can be filtered or shuffled per section.

  3. 3

    Plan the exam

    The exam prep page surfaces the highest-yield topics, common question patterns from past papers, and a day-by-day plan you can adapt to your timeline.
